Frequently Asked Questions

Why does professional window installation matter?

Professional installation ensures your windows fit correctly, seal tightly and perform as designed — preventing drafts, leaks, moisture intrusion and energy loss over time.

How do I choose the right windows for my home?

Consider energy ratings (U-value, SHGC), frame material (vinyl, fiberglass, wood), glass package, climate, and the room’s purpose. A reputable installer can match the right options to your home and lifestyle.

How long does a window installation take?

Most window installations are completed in a single day. Larger whole-home projects may take several days. You’ll receive an accurate timeline during your consultation.

Will new windows really lower my energy bills?

Yes. Properly installed energy-efficient windows reduce drafts and heat transfer, often lowering heating and cooling costs by 10–25%.

What sets a trusted local window installer apart?

Look for proper licensing and insurance, employee crews (not subcontractors), manufacturer authorization, transparent estimates and a track record of satisfied local homeowners.
